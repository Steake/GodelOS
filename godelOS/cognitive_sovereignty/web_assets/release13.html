Room to think.
Something to return to.

V13 connects persistent positions, associative wandering, bounded inquiries and invitations to the conversation you can actually have with the agent.

Try a developing thread

  1. Ask a question you genuinely disagree about. Invite a position with reasons and a revision condition.
  2. Use Follow a thought. Ordinary cognition keeps its existing streaming reply.
  3. Open What is developing? to inspect positions, associations and inquiry evidence.
  4. Challenge the same position. See whether its direction, confidence or reasons actually change.
  5. Return to an invitation. The composer preserves your existing draft.

Background cycles also advance queued retrieval inquiries, one fresh-model branch per scheduled tick. A queued operator message takes priority.

What actually ran

32 DeepSeek calls across four short developmental histories: 24 conversational/thought episodes and eight probe calls. All 24 episodes delivered replies. Three sets of structured updates were quarantined, with their raw evidence retained. Four two-branch retrieval inquiries completed.

The final build recorded one refinement of a position and no categorical stance reversal. These were prompted smoke experiments, not a powered test of autonomy or consciousness.

Alpha boundaries

The executable inquiry is currently a fixed stance-retrieval protocol. The original agent question is retained alongside the narrower compiled question. Arbitrary proposed experiments and code changes are not executed by this feature.

Visual browser verification was blocked in the build environment. Responsive light/dark styles are included, but this release does not claim a completed visual QA pass. The underlying V12 research remains accessible in the historical release desk.
